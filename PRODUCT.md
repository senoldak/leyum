# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Children aged 4–12 and their parents/teachers reading together or independently in Norway and Turkey. They arrive on smartphones, tablets, or desktop browsers, often seeking engaging stories, curiosities, nature facts, or bedtime reading.

## Product Purpose

leyum is a bilingual (Norwegian Bokmål `nb-NO` and Turkish `tr-TR`) digital magazine for children. It delivers whimsical, educational, safe, and beautifully illustrated stories, scientific curiosities, nature exploration, and interactive brain teasers.

## Positioning

A 100% non-commercial, advertisement-free, safe haven for curious minds. No tracking, no paywalls, no sponsored content. It treats children as intelligent explorers and readers with respect, wonder, and warmth.

## Operating Context

- Bilingual static site built with Astro on Cloudflare Pages.
- Clean separation of routes: `/no/` (Norwegian) and `/tr/` (Turkish) with direct language toggle.
- Content managed via Markdown collections and Sveltia CMS (`/admin`).
- Fast, secure static hosting with strict Content Security Policy (`public/_headers`).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **leyum**, always lowercase.
- Slogan / Character: Meraklı ve rengarenk çocuk dergisi / Et nysgjerrig og fargerikt barnemagasin.
- Strictly non-commercial: No ads, no affiliate banners, no corporate trackers.
- Warm, playful, high-craft editorial design that feels like a treasured print volume brought to life digitally.

## Product Principles

1. **Child-first clarity & wonder**: Typography, layout, and contrast must be effortless for early and developing readers, yet delightful and imaginative.
2. **True editorial dignity**: Avoid cheap clipart and generic SaaS patterns; embrace tactile, layered storybook aesthetics, rich paper warmth, custom SVG icons, and editorial pacing.
3. **Interactive joy**: Subtle, organic interactions (tactile buttons, discovery toggles, playful star mascots) without overstimulation or chaotic distractions.
4. **Performance & security**: Blazing fast static loading with zero external tracking scripts.
