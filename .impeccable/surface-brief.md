# Surface Brief: leyum Home & Reader Surface

## Scope and visitor mode
Mode: Read & Experience. The child and parent explore stories, curiosities, activities, and teasers.

## Audience & Job
Children 4–12 and parents seeking captivating, safe, advert-free stories and curiosities in Norwegian and Turkish.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A tactile, storybook-folio editorial publication that treats children's curiosities with wonder and craftsmanship, entirely refusing generic SaaS card grids, lazy emoji iconography, and cold flat templates.
OWN-WORLD: Sunlit warm cream ground (#FAF7F0), Nordic blueberry navy (#182238), warm coral amber (#E65A28), cloud sage (#2E7D5B), and honey buttercup (#F59E0B). Tailored bespoke SVG icons, textured paper borders, hand-drawn star talisman, and dual typography: display storybook serif/slab with friendly geometric humanist body.
STORY: The reader steps into a living story volume where every section is a distinct discovery chamber: lead tale, curiosity cabinet, interactive riddle chamber, and animal observatory.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Asymmetric illustrated masthead with bilingual weather/season ribbon, tactile star emblem, hero story featured in an open-book frame with category seal and age indicator, flanked by the curiosity explorer tray.
FORM: Botanical Folio / Layered Storybook Folio (seed key a9fd5157).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
