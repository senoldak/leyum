# Design System: leyum (Storybook Folio)

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## World Thesis
A tactile, storybook-folio editorial publication that treats children's curiosities with wonder, dignity, and craftsmanship. Completely refuses lazy emojis, generic SaaS card templates, and cold sterile boxes.

## Color Strategy: Committed Editorial Palette
- **Canvas Ground**: `#FAF6ED` (Warm storybook parchment)
- **Paper Surface**: `#FFFFFF` (Clean reading background)
- **Primary Ink**: `#1C2333` (Nordic blueberry deep navy, ratio > 10:1 on canvas)
- **Body Text**: `#344054` (Warm reading charcoal)
- **Brand Terracotta**: `#D9480F` (Lead accent, tactile call-to-actions)
- **Brand Talisman Gold**: `#F59F00` (Star mascot, discovery moments)

### Category Inks & Tints (Calibrated ≥ 4.5:1 contrast)
- 🚀 **Meraklı Kaşif (oppdager)**: Surface `#EBF5FB`, Ink `#004578`
- 📖 **Masal & Hikaye (eventyr)**: Surface `#F6EEFD`, Ink `#4E138A`
- 🎨 **Etkinlik & Sanat (aktiviteter)**: Surface `#FFF0E6`, Ink `#9C2E00`
- 🧩 **Zeka & Bilmece (gater)**: Surface `#E6FCF5`, Ink `#086B4D`
- 🌍 **Doğa & Hayvanlar (natur)**: Surface `#EBFBEE`, Ink `#1B5E20`

## Typography Hierarchy
- **Display & Headings**: `Fraunces`, Georgia, serif (optical size tailored, distinct warmth)
- **Body & Meta**: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, system-ui, sans-serif (crisp geometric humanist clarity)

## Geometry & Elevation
- Rounded tactile radii: `8px`, `14px`, `22px`, `32px`
- Deep folio elevation: `0 10px 30px -8px rgba(90, 78, 60, 0.12)`
- Non-generic browser theming: Custom warm selection (`#FED7AA`), styled scrollbars, keyboard focus rings.
